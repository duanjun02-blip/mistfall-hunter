August 19, 2026 update

On August 19, Bellring Games shipped a no-downtime update to fix a batch of known issues and tune several classes. The game client must be updated through the platform store afterward (official Steam announcement, Aug 19, 2026).

New additions & optimizations

  • Scavenger Squad refresh: after completing a task and claiming its rewards, the required materials across all modes will refresh.
  • Mist Lord room invulnerability: a brief period of invulnerability is now granted when entering a Mist Lord's room.
  • Quality of life: text and localization strings were optimized to prevent UI clipping; a new Favorite function was added; Star Silver refining has an improved quick-operation flow; taking fall damage now triggers unique controller vibration.
  • Server performance: dedicated server frame-rate monitoring revealed that refreshing chimes and treasure chests can occasionally trigger excessive computation loads. These specific issues will be resolved in upcoming updates.

Class balance & combat changes

  • Mercenary: reduced stagger duration inflicted on enemies; increased energy consumption and cooldowns for several talents/skills; reduced shield value, cooldown-reduction effects and PvP damage on affected skills; smaller hitbox on one skill; several other cooldowns slightly reduced. Also fixed Hammer cooldown-reduction talent failing after being revived, a talent staying active after weapon swaps, and a Sword & Shield combo-chain issue.
  • Withered Knight: increased movement distance and attack speed of the first Greatsword light strike; increased movement distance on several skills and forward dodge; charged heavy strikes now gain Super Armor during the release animation; sword-wave projectile speed increased and now applies a slow while reducing enemy dodge distance; slightly reduced energy consumption and cooldown on an affected skill.
  • Shadowstrix: fixed a talent triggered by dodge failing to grant invincibility frames, Super Armor performance on a talent, an interrupt action failing in specific circumstances, and aerial heavy strikes being unable to change direction. A wind-up animation was added to one talent.
  • Sorcerer: fixed the Sorcerer - Elemental talent failing after being revived from a downed state.
  • Seer: fixed two Blasphemers failing to trigger Super Armor Clash under a talent's Zeal state.
  • Gem affixes: fixed an affix effect malfunctioning when its level was upgraded.

Critical & Soul Harvest fixes

  • Fixed Smuggler Woodlings failing to spawn in a match after Soul Harvest began.
  • Fixed Gyldening damage calculations functioning abnormally in specific circumstances.
  • Fixed the Soulgnawer's missing textures in certain scenarios.
  • Fixed the Mistchaser Shop limits failing to refresh after completing the Soul Harvest ritual.
  • Fixed special Soul Incubators spawned during the purification prayer phase incorrectly dropping Soul Cocoons when looted.

The full announcement also lists ~25 additional UI, audio, terrain, inventory and trio-mode fixes. See the official Steam Community announcement for the complete list.

August 15, 2026 update

On August 15, Bellring Games announced a server optimization update plus Soul Harvest launch rewards (official Steam announcement, Aug 15, 2026).

Server stutter fix

  • One cause of intermittent stuttering (lag, character teleporting) was traced to cloud server CPU hyper-threading.
  • Configuration adjustments were rolled out across all servers on August 13-14; monitoring shows stutters are now less frequent. Optimization is ongoing.

Soul Harvest rewards

  • Complete Soul Harvest runs to earn exclusive tokens, exchangeable at the Mistchaser Shop for rare rewards - including the Season-exclusive Holy-quality collectible Soulbound Serpent Crystal, Holy Material Selection Chest, Moonlight Nectar, and Soul Coins.
  • The Fate Chart Shop now also offers Moonlight Nectar and a Legendary Material Selection Chest, with more rare rewards added as the season progresses.
  • Login reward: log in before August 22, 2026, 00:00 UTC+0 to claim Moonlight Nectar x5, Pro Combat Bag x2 and Advanced Combat Bag x2 via in-game mail. (See our Combat Bags guide for how to get more.)

August 12, 2026 update

The August 12 Live Update shipped as a no-downtime patch — afterwards you update your client via the app store.

New gameplay: Soul Harvest (Hunt Rituals)

  • Soul Harvest is live — Season 1's headline mode, alongside a new stage of the Fate Chart. It triggers randomly inside Hallowgrove and Brandrgarde matches: Soul Incubators carrying Soul Cocoons spawn along with a Fate Altar; higher Gyldening Intensity raises the trigger chance.
  • Cocoon resonance: holding a Soul Cocoon reveals all other cocoon locations on the map — and reveals you to other holders.
  • Ritual: submit 2 Soul Cocoons at the Fate Altar to start the purification prayer; or give 1 Soul Cocoon to Richie, the in-match merchant, for a special team reward.
  • Full mechanic breakdown: Soul Harvest guide.

Other additions

  • World Chat now has an "Add to Blocklist" function.
  • [Xbox] Added real-time voice chat.

  • Einherjar Mist Lord challenges opened — the hardest boss encounters are now available to challenge.

August 7, 2026 online update

  • Fixed a Sword & Shield charge bug (Mercenary) that affected gameplay.

FAQ

  • Where do I read official patch notes? Steam Community announcements for Mistfall Hunter (app 3282300) — the devs post full notes there.
