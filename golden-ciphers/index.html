Mistfall Hunter Golden Ciphers Guide — All Words & Correct NPCs

Golden Ciphers are the Legendary-quality event currency of the Gylden Rush event. Loot them in Gyldenmist runs, extract safely, then hand each cipher to the one correct NPC in Windrest Camp to unlock the bonus Epic reward. Wrong NPC = you still get paid, but the bonus is gone. This guide lists every cipher word and the NPC who deciphers it.

How to get Golden Ciphers

Golden Ciphers drop as Legendary-quality scrolls during a run. You must survive and extract to carry one back to camp — if you die, other players can loot it from your body like any other drop. Confirmed sources:

  • Locked Chests — the most reliable loot source; open every one you find in Brandrgarde and Hallowgrove.
  • Unidentified Items — picking these up can roll into a Golden Cipher once identified.
  • Golden Envoys — during event hunt hours, a mysterious group called The Unbound carries ciphers into Gyldenmist-affected zones. Kill the Golden Envoy and extract with what it drops.
  • Marked enemies (purple brands) and the containers they guard.
  • Monster drops — regular and hard enemies in dense zones such as Witchery Woods and Hastine's Fall.

Best farm routes

  • Hallowgrove: check Einherjar Ship Grave and Supreme Hall; around Divine Anchor, clear Harpies and Abominations — the highest cipher density outside chest routes.
  • Brandrgarde: run a high-monster-density loop and open every chest. Consistency beats risk.
  • Golden rule: the moment a cipher drops, prioritize a clean extraction over clearing another room. One survived extract is worth more than a full clear that ends in death.

All cipher words & correct NPCs

Every cipher (Prayer, Lost, Old World, or Nameless) prints two lines of clue words. Match those words to the table below, then take the cipher to that NPC in Windrest Camp and choose Decipher.

How to decipher a Golden Cipher

  1. Reach character level 5. The Decipher interaction only appears on camp NPCs once you hit level 5. If nobody will accept your cipher before then, that's expected — keep it and level up.
  2. Extract with the cipher in your inventory. Dying before extraction loses it unless it's in a protected pocket.
  3. Return to Windrest Camp and read the two lines of words on the cipher.
  4. Walk to the matching NPC and choose Decipher (not the shop, forge or quest option).
  5. Confirm the exchange. The correct NPC grants a bonus item on top of the standard payout — the bonus has a high chance to be Epic rarity.

Don't guess. Handing a cipher to any NPC still completes the exchange, but you only get the base payout and the bonus item is gone forever. Use the table above every time.

All rewards & milestones

Deciphering ciphers rolls from a reward pool that includes cosmetics, Gyldenblod, weapon boxes, game keys, season-pass rewards and entries into the physical grand prizes.

  • Decipher 10 ciphers → Strike It Rich emote (automatic)
  • Decipher 20 ciphers → Golden Woodling Head cosmetic (automatic)

Duplicate cosmetics automatically convert to Gyldenblod. If you're chasing the milestones, plan your runs in batches while the event is live.

FAQ

  • How do I know which NPC is correct? Match the two lines of words printed on the cipher to the table above. If you pick wrong, the exchange completes but you lose the bonus item.
  • Why won't any NPC let me decipher? You're below character level 5. Level up and the Decipher option appears.
  • Can I lose a Golden Cipher? Yes — if you die before extracting and it's not in a protected pocket, other players can take it.
  • When does the event end? Grand-prize eligibility closes Aug 10, 23:59 UTC; cipher drops and deciphering continue until Aug 12, 17:00 UTC.
  • Are Golden Ciphers available outside the event? No — they're a limited-time event currency and only drop during Gylden Rush.
