Blackarrow Build — Hunter's Arrow DoT & Spacing

The Blackarrow is Mistfall Hunter's ranged DPS/scout: high mobility, safe kiting and strong output at almost any range. The Hunter's Arrow build below is the strongest all-round option in 2026 — a fast, DoT-heavy playstyle built around spacing and chasing, and one of the easiest classes for newcomers to get value from.

Why Hunter's Arrow?

The Hunter's Arrow build leans into Blackarrow's strength: damage-over-time (DoT) arrows that let you keep pressure up while repositioning. Guides describe it as almost a melee-range bow build — you stay close enough to threaten, but always mobile enough to escape. It is seriously strong in both solo and trio, and multiple outlets (Skycoach, Mobalytics) rank Blackarrow S-tier: Mobalytics puts Blackarrow in S tier for both trio and solo, and GamesRadar places it in A tier. The devs are watching its power (see patch history), so re-check numbers after patches.

Loadout note: swap Paralysis Arrow → Spore Arrow when fighting melee-heavy lobbies. Against a good Seer or Withered Knight you lose extended trades — keep moving and stay just out of melee reach.

Talents

Core talents for the Hunter's Arrow build: Pursuit Mark, Mark Pursuer, Blood Infection, Powerful Scattershot, Battle Fed, Lasting Pain, Dodge Weapon Shot and Laceration. The theme is simple: mark targets, extend your DoTs, and make every dodge a weapon. Exact talent order depends on your playstyle (pure DoT vs. burst follow-up) — the community guides below cover both variants.

Affix priority

Affixes come from gems socketed into gear. For the Hunter's Arrow Blackarrow, follow this order:

  1. Vitality — more skill energy to keep the arrows flowing.
  2. Elusive — better dodges; spacing is the Blackarrow's whole game.
  3. Valor — the reliable damage affix for nearly every class.
  4. Curse — strengthens your Barbed/Bloodfly DoT damage; take it once your survivability feels comfortable.

PvP & counters

  • Strengths: safe ranged DPS, excellent kiting, straightforward to play, strong in PvE and PvP.
  • Counters: a good Seer (cleanse + healing) or Withered Knight (closing power) is your worst matchup — play around range and terrain.
  • Don't overcommit: your damage comes from DoT ticking while you stay alive. If an enemy forces you into a face-to-face trade, you're usually losing it.
  • In trios: sit behind your melee, apply marks, and punish anyone who tries to dive your backline.

Javelin (second weapon)

The Blackarrow's second weapon type — the Javelin — was teased in the official DevNote #8 (Aug 20, 2026): it is "currently in full production" and the developers shared first concept art. No release date or skill list has been announced yet. When it ships, this page will be updated with the Javelin build; until then, the Bow / Hunter's Arrow build is the playable meta.

Patch history & what's next

  • Aug 6, 2026: Steel Arrow no longer knocks enemies back on uncharged shots; fully charged shots knock back slightly less — a control nerf, not a damage nerf.
  • Aug 12, 2026: cooldowns increased on Mysticfly Arrow and Steel Arrow, and slightly on Dodge Power Shot; Sonic Arrow cooldown greatly reduced; Blackarrow's solo-mode PvP damage multiplier reduced (PvE unchanged).
  • DevNote #8 (Aug 20, upcoming): the devs say ranged burst is already being moderately nerfed and they are evaluating ranged mobility and terrain amplification — expect more tuning for Blackarrow. The Javelin weapon is in production.

Full context: patch notes.

Solo & trio tips

  • Keep your spacing. Your whole kit rewards staying at range — kite sideways, not back, when pressured.
  • Stack your DoTs then escape. Barbed + Bloodfly on a target and disengage; the damage lands while they chase.
  • Shadow Step is precious. Don't burn it to move faster across open ground — save it for the engage or the escape.
  • In trios, mark the diver. As soon as an enemy Shadowstrix or Mercenary commits on your team, stack arrows on them and force them out.
  • Newcomer-friendly: Blackarrow is one of the easiest classes to pick up (Skycoach calls it very easy to understand) — a great second class after the beginner guide.
