Does Mistfall Hunter Wipe? (Season Reset Explained)

Yes — Mistfall Hunter has a partial seasonal reset. The developers confirmed the framework in DevNote #8 (Aug 20, 2026): at the start of each new season, character levels, warehouse supplies and select camp buildings reset, while long-term progression (like the Collection Pedestal) carries over — and you get compensation for the seasonal assets you lose.

Short answer

Yes, Mistfall Hunter wipes at the start of each season — but it is a partial reset, not a full account wipe:

  • Resets: character levels, warehouse supplies, and select camp buildings.
  • Carries over: long-term progression systems such as the Collection Pedestal.
  • Compensation: you get a portion of your seasonal assets back, exchangeable for key resources (Pip's Rare Pouch, Unbound Holy Collectibles, Holy Soulcatch Nets, exclusive cosmetics and Combat Bags).
  • Bonus: exclusive honorary rewards based on your net asset worth from the previous season.

This was officially confirmed in the developers' DevNote #8 (Aug 20, 2026). Exact compensation items, trade-in ratios and the full reset list will be published near the end of Season 1.

What carries over & compensation

To soften the wipe, the developers return a portion of your seasonal assets as compensation. These can be exchanged for critical resources, including:

  • Pip's Rare Pouch — a seasonal storage/utility item (unlocks after 50 core Season Tasks, usable all season).
  • Unbound Holy Collectibles and Holy Soulcatch Nets.
  • Exclusive cosmetics and various tiers of Combat Bags.

In addition, based on your net asset worth from the previous season, you receive exclusive honorary rewards at the start of the new season. The developers stressed that supplies you worked hard to collect will "not completely vanish into thin air." See our Combat Bags guide for how Combat Bag tiers work.

Why Mistfall Hunter wipes

The developers explained their reasoning in DevNote #8: if every asset carried over fully into each new season, they would have to drastically tighten drop rates and slow resource acquisition just to keep the in-game economy stable. Instead, the wipe lets every player enjoy a fresh start each season, fast-paced progression and relatively high-impact drop rates. It also levels the playing field between returning veterans and newcomers — a design choice the developers say they locked in early, drawing on seasonal operational data from successful extraction games.

When is the wipe?

The wipe happens at the start of each new season. For Season 1 (the current "Soul Hunt" season, which launched with Soul Harvest on Aug 12), that means the wipe would come with the Season 2 start — Season 2 hasn't been dated yet. The developers will publish a full breakdown of the wipe (exact compensation items, trade-in ratios and reset categories) near the end of Season 1. Follow our patch notes page for updates.

FAQ

  • Does Mistfall Hunter wipe? Yes — a partial seasonal reset is confirmed: character levels, warehouse supplies and select camp buildings reset each season, with compensation and long-term progression (Collection Pedestal) carrying over.
  • Will I lose all my gear? Not everything. Warehouse supplies reset, but you receive compensation that can be exchanged for key resources, and the Collection Pedestal is not reset.
  • What carries over between seasons? Long-term progression systems such as the Collection Pedestal. The developers also return a portion of your seasonal assets as compensation.
  • When does the Season 1 wipe happen? At the start of Season 2 — Season 2's date hasn't been announced. Full wipe details come near the end of Season 1.
  • Why does the game wipe? To keep the economy stable while preserving fast progression and high drop rates, and to give everyone a fresh start each season.
