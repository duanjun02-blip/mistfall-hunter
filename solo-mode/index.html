Mistfall Hunter Solo Mode — Release Date & What We Know

Solo Mode for Brandrgarde is officially confirmed. It arrives in mid-September 2026 as part of Season 1 Phase 2, for both Normal and Cataclysm difficulties. It was the single most-requested feature from the community since launch.

What the devs confirmed

On August 5, 2026, Bellring Games celebrated the 1,000,000-player milestone and confirmed in an official announcement:

"We can now confirm that we'll be introducing Solo Mode for both the Normal and Cataclysm difficulties of Brandrgarde in the middle of Season 1 in September."

Key points straight from the announcement:

  • Map: Brandrgarde only (the higher-difficulty extraction map).
  • Difficulties: both Normal and Cataclysm will get their own solo queue.
  • Timing: middle of Season 1 (September 2026), shipped with Season 1 Phase 2 content.
  • Trade-off acknowledged: the devs note that two new modes will split the matchmaking queue and create fresh class-balance challenges in solo play. They'll keep evaluating and will communicate findings before further adjustments.

Normal & Cataclysm details

  • Normal Brandrgarde solo: the standard extraction experience without other players on the map. You still fight Gyldenmist-corrupted monsters and NPC threats, but you won't run into rival Gyldhunters.
  • Cataclysm Brandrgarde solo: the same solo queue at the highest difficulty — higher-gear requirements, tougher enemies and stricter extract rules, but no player-vs-player competition for loot.
  • Matchmaking split: adding solo queues splits the existing player pool. Expect the devs to monitor queue times and tweak the split before fully rolling it out.

What changes for solo players

  • Extraction is no longer a PvP gamble on Brandrgarde: the core complaint that drove the feature — losing your hard-earned loot to a rival Gyldhunter — disappears in solo mode.
  • Class balance shifts: builds that rely on teammate support (e.g., sword & shield Mercenary tanking) play differently alone. The devs explicitly flagged solo class balance as an area they'll evaluate.
  • Gear pressure stays: solo mode doesn't lower the gear-score requirements or bag limits of Cataclysm — bring your best kit.
  • Season tasks: Phase 2 also unlocks the new seasonal tasks, so solo players get their own progression path at the same time.

Can you play solo right now?

Not yet — the official solo queue arrives mid-September. In the meantime, solo-friendly options that already exist:

  • Hallowgrove runs: lower-difficulty extraction map with weaker competition; practical for grinding gear solo today.
  • Einherjar mode: the boss-focused mode (including the new Dried Flower Knots reward from Mist Lords) is more solo-viable than Brandrgarde PvPvE.
  • Queue at off-peak hours: fewer rivals on Brandrgarde means a de-facto solo experience more often — but it's not guaranteed.

Don't get caught out: until solo mode ships, everything you bring into Brandrgarde can still be taken by other players. If you want zero-PvP extraction, wait for the official mode or stick to Hallowgrove.

FAQ

  • When does Solo Mode come out? Mid-September 2026, with Season 1 Phase 2. No exact date yet.
  • Which map gets solo mode? Brandrgarde — both Normal and Cataclysm difficulties.
  • Is Hallowgrove getting solo mode? Not announced. The confirmation covers Brandrgarde only.
  • Will solo mode remove PvP entirely? It removes rival Gyldhunters from your queue, but the map's monsters and threats remain.
  • Does solo mode cost anything? No — it's part of the free Season 1 update.
