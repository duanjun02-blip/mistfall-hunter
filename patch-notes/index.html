August 27, 2026 — Server Maintenance Update

On August 27 (Steam Community announcement), Bellring Games announced a server maintenance window for August 28, 06:30 UTC+0 (approximately 2 hours; matchmaking queues closed early from 06:00 UTC+0), after which the game client must be updated through the platform store. The same announcement details a large set of Season Task / Contract Task difficulty reductions, new content, and another class-balance pass. It is a live update, not a season reset.

Season Tasks

  • Monster-slaying tasks: reduced kill-count requirements and lowered difficulty-mode restrictions.
  • Defeat-Gyldhunter tasks: "execute" requirements changed to "defeat", and the required target count was reduced.
  • Equipment tasks: equipment value restrictions changed to equipment rarity requirements.
  • UI: task description layouts optimized; objectives that support team-wide progression sharing now have visual indicators.

Contract

  • Reduced completion counts required for most tasks.
  • Removed tasks that required searching specific containers.
  • Simplified tactical-item tasks — now completable by using any tactical item.
  • Individual Daily Task reward increased from 30 to 40 Contract Sigils.

New content & optimizations

  • Added a new Oceania server.
  • Brandrgarde: removed some low-level monsters positioned too densely or near the edges, improving the PvE experience.
  • Removed the display of enemy player names above characters during matches (to reduce cross-teaming violations).
  • The inventory stack limit of a targeted item was changed to 1 to prevent griefing/snatching by malicious players.
  • Reduced server-side rubberbanding triggered by branch skills right after a dodge, and by destroying breakable objects via movement/skills.
  • Blocklist limit increased to 100; chat got a "Scroll to Bottom" button; recruitment messages can now be displayed in World Chat; a system performance diagnostic pop-up was added; purchases priced in the Overpayment Range now show a secondary confirmation.

Class balance

  • Mercenary: removed invincibility frames (i-frames) from dodges performed under the effects of several talents and increased their Energy consumption; added a short internal cooldown to cooldown-reduction effects on certain talents (preventing instant resets when hitting multiple targets); adjusted Energy-consumption timing on two skills (Energy now consumed at startup, single-use cost reduced — aimed at eliminating zero-cost Hammer charge/cancel pressure); increased the cooldown and animation speed of other skills; fixed a light-strike chaining bug.
  • Withered Knight: fixed missing footstep audio under specific circumstances; fixed a long-range dash attack that traveled too fast and could launch players into the sky.
  • Shadowstrix: the speed-boost effect of one skill now only applies when moving toward enemies; reduced damage and movement distance on skills to rein in Super Armor counterattacks; fixed abnormal movement from dodge+jump combos and abnormal talent stacking.
  • Sorcerer: reduced PvP damage on one skill; increased Energy recovery when releasing uncharged; greatly reduced damage on another skill; rebalanced damage distribution across channel stages; increased channeling time to reach higher stages when un-buffed; reduced cooldowns; greatly reduced the movement-speed slow and dodge-distance reduction effects.
  • Blackarrow: reduced the duration extension a talent applies to immobilize/crowd-control debuffs; increased damage on one skill; fixed a climbing-ladder visual issue.
  • Seer: fixed a skill that could remove enemy characters' invulnerability when leaving a Mist Lord's room.
  • Tactical items & gems: an item series gained auto target-seeking (plus a stronger speed boost and dodge-energy reduction); a thrown item's hit radius and downed-state duration were increased; fixed a gem affix that could stack abnormally.

Other fixes

  • Visual fixes in the Contract Rewards exchange screen, chat window, controller layout menu and event screen; rope and invisible-wall collision fixes in Hallowgrove; terrain collision fixes in Brandrgarde; a Soul Ferry extraction texture fix; background-music fixes; a "Match Found" audio override fix; a physics fix that prevented characters launching into the sky after high-speed movement.

August 28, 2026 — Fair Play Penalty Announcement

On August 28 (Steam Community announcement), Bellring Games confirmed that the penalty algorithm for Frequent Temporary Teaming in Matches was optimized this week and that penalties resumed as of August 27. The principle is unchanged: targeting teaming behavior that causes the most severe harm to PvP fairness. Players who spot suspected cheaters or illegal teaming are asked to report in-game. A penalty list (obfuscated account names) accompanies the announcement.

August 20, 2026 — DevNote #8

The developers' eighth Developer Update (Aug 20, 2026) confirmed a 1.5 million players milestone, revealed the seasonal reset (wipe) framework, previewed a class-balance roadmap, addressed Season Task pacing, and teased Blackarrow's next weapon. It is a roadmap/design update, not a client patch.

Player milestone

  • 1.5 million+ Gyldhunters have arrived in Windrest (up from 1M in week one). Full numbers: player count page.

Seasonal reset (wipe) framework

  • What resets each new season: character levels, warehouse supplies, and select camp buildings.
  • Seasonal compensation: a portion of your seasonal assets is returned as compensation, exchangeable for critical resources (Pip's Rare Pouch, Unbound Holy Collectibles, Holy Soulcatch Nets, exclusive cosmetics, and various Combat Bag tiers).
  • What carries over: long-term progression systems such as the Collection Pedestal are not reset.
  • Bonus rewards: exclusive honorary rewards based on your previous season's net asset worth.
  • Exact compensation items, trade-in ratios and reset categories will be detailed near the end of Season 1.
  • Full breakdown: Does Mistfall Hunter Wipe? — Season reset explained.

Class-balance roadmap (upcoming)

  • Shadowstrix: the "hatchet-running" loot playstyle stays, but the devs plan to remove Super Armor from several talents/skills and rein in its map-mobility + front-line combat combination.
  • Mercenary (Sword & Shield): both builds are overperforming. Red Shield's Deflect is being adjusted (costs, precision window, miss risk); the Sword build already received moderate skill/damage tweaks.
  • Mercenary (Hammer): in line with expectations, with possible minor tweaks later.
  • Withered Knight: Quick Shield Bash is fine; Enhanced Shield Bash is underperforming and likely gets a significant Season 2 buff. Greatsword basic attacks were recently buffed.
  • Ranged classes (Blackarrow & Sorcerer): moderate burst nerfs already started; mobility and terrain amplification are being evaluated for further tuning.

Season Tasks pacing

  • The devs apologized: 152 tasks to unlock all end-game rewards felt overwhelming.
  • Key numbers: Pip's Rare Pouch needs only 50 core tasks; the Legendary Nether Ferry skin needs 93 tasks.
  • Future seasons will drastically cut the task count, improve pacing, and remove mandatory three-player requirements.

Teaser

  • Blackarrow's Javelin (second weapon type) is in full production — concept art shared.

August 19, 2026 update

On August 19, Bellring Games shipped a no-downtime update to fix a batch of known issues and tune several classes. The game client must be updated through the platform store afterward (official Steam announcement, Aug 19, 2026).

New additions & optimizations

  • Scavenger Squad refresh: after completing a task and claiming its rewards, the required materials across all modes will refresh.
  • Mist Lord room invulnerability: a brief period of invulnerability is now granted when entering a Mist Lord's room.
  • Quality of life: text and localization strings were optimized to prevent UI clipping; a new Favorite function was added; Star Silver refining has an improved quick-operation flow; taking fall damage now triggers unique controller vibration.
  • Server performance: dedicated server frame-rate monitoring revealed that refreshing chimes and treasure chests can occasionally trigger excessive computation loads. These specific issues will be resolved in upcoming updates.

Class balance & combat changes

  • Mercenary: reduced stagger duration inflicted on enemies; increased energy consumption and cooldowns for several talents/skills; reduced shield value, cooldown-reduction effects and PvP damage on affected skills; smaller hitbox on one skill; several other cooldowns slightly reduced. Also fixed Hammer cooldown-reduction talent failing after being revived, a talent staying active after weapon swaps, and a Sword & Shield combo-chain issue.
  • Withered Knight: increased movement distance and attack speed of the first Greatsword light strike; increased movement distance on several skills and forward dodge; charged heavy strikes now gain Super Armor during the release animation; sword-wave projectile speed increased and now applies a slow while reducing enemy dodge distance; slightly reduced energy consumption and cooldown on an affected skill.
  • Shadowstrix: fixed a talent triggered by dodge failing to grant invincibility frames, Super Armor performance on a talent, an interrupt action failing in specific circumstances, and aerial heavy strikes being unable to change direction. A wind-up animation was added to one talent.
  • Sorcerer: fixed the Sorcerer - Elemental talent failing after being revived from a downed state.
  • Seer: fixed two Blasphemers failing to trigger Super Armor Clash under a talent's Zeal state.
  • Gem affixes: fixed an affix effect malfunctioning when its level was upgraded.

Critical & Soul Harvest fixes

  • Fixed Smuggler Woodlings failing to spawn in a match after Soul Harvest began.
  • Fixed Gyldening damage calculations functioning abnormally in specific circumstances.
  • Fixed the Soulgnawer's missing textures in certain scenarios.
  • Fixed the Mistchaser Shop limits failing to refresh after completing the Soul Harvest ritual.
  • Fixed special Soul Incubators spawned during the purification prayer phase incorrectly dropping Soul Cocoons when looted.

The full announcement also lists ~25 additional UI, audio, terrain, inventory and trio-mode fixes. See the official Steam Community announcement for the complete list.

August 15, 2026 update

On August 15, Bellring Games announced a server optimization update plus Soul Harvest launch rewards (official Steam announcement, Aug 15, 2026).

Server stutter fix

  • One cause of intermittent stuttering (lag, character teleporting) was traced to cloud server CPU hyper-threading.
  • Configuration adjustments were rolled out across all servers on August 13-14; monitoring shows stutters are now less frequent. Optimization is ongoing.

Soul Harvest rewards

  • Complete Soul Harvest runs to earn exclusive tokens, exchangeable at the Mistchaser Shop for rare rewards - including the Season-exclusive Holy-quality collectible Soulbound Serpent Crystal, Holy Material Selection Chest, Moonlight Nectar, and Soul Coins.
  • The Fate Chart Shop now also offers Moonlight Nectar and a Legendary Material Selection Chest, with more rare rewards added as the season progresses.
  • Login reward: log in before August 22, 2026, 00:00 UTC+0 to claim Moonlight Nectar x5, Pro Combat Bag x2 and Advanced Combat Bag x2 via in-game mail. (See our Combat Bags guide for how to get more.)

August 12, 2026 update

The August 12 Live Update shipped as a no-downtime patch — afterwards you update your client via the app store.

New gameplay: Soul Harvest (Hunt Rituals)

  • Soul Harvest is live — Season 1's headline mode, alongside a new stage of the Fate Chart. It triggers randomly inside Hallowgrove and Brandrgarde matches: Soul Incubators carrying Soul Cocoons spawn along with a Fate Altar; higher Gyldening Intensity raises the trigger chance.
  • Cocoon resonance: holding a Soul Cocoon reveals all other cocoon locations on the map — and reveals you to other holders.
  • Ritual: submit 2 Soul Cocoons at the Fate Altar to start the purification prayer; or give 1 Soul Cocoon to Richie, the in-match merchant, for a special team reward.
  • Full mechanic breakdown: Soul Harvest guide.

Other additions

  • World Chat now has an "Add to Blocklist" function.
  • [Xbox] Added real-time voice chat.

FAQ

  • Where do I read official patch notes? Steam Community announcements for Mistfall Hunter (app 3282300) — the devs post full notes there.
  • How often does the game update? Frequent: launch hotfix Jul 30, balance patch Aug 6, online update Aug 7, and the August 12 client patch that launched Soul Harvest — plus weekly content drops from the roadmap.
  • Do I need to reinstall to update? No — client patches download through Steam/console store automatically.

  • Is solo mode in this patch? Not yet — it arrives mid-September with Season 1 Phase 2.

Yes — a partial seasonal reset is confirmed: character levels, warehouse supplies and select camp buildings reset each season, with compensation and long-term progression (Collection Pedestal) carrying over.
