August 6, 2026 patch

New content

  • Dried Flower Knots now drop — new key item from Mist Lords in Einherjar mode. Used to directly activate the Mist Lord's Einherjar mode in Gyldening Intensity: Chaos. See our full Dried Flower Knots guide.
  • Einherjar Mist Lord challenges opened — the hardest boss encounters are now available to challenge.

Class balance

ClassChanges
BlackarrowSteel Arrow: removed the impact effect from uncharged shots; slightly reduced the impact of fully charged shots. Fixed displacement-after-dodge and stealth-aim collision issues.
Withered KnightSlightly reduced Energy cost of Sprinting Slash and Withering Mark. Fixed skills clipping through obstacles, interruption by Inspect, and Thorn Guide dragging NPCs.
MercenaryFixed Sword & Shield triggering auto-blocks in specific situations.
SorcererFixed Stardust playstyle unable to use a charged Meteor Charm in specific situations.

Notable fixes

  • PS/Xbox friends list crash fixed.
  • Hallowgrove Gyldenmist dealing no damage in specific situations fixed.
  • Stackable items stacking incorrectly fixed.
  • Gem affixes causing mid-match crashes fixed.
  • "Allowed Combat Bags" display for Brandrgarde Cataclysm fixed.
  • Weekly task refresh times in Contracts and Fate Chart fixed.
  • "Iron and Fire" achievement now completable.
  • Plus ~25 more stability, UI, audio and interaction fixes.

August 7, 2026 online update

  • Fixed a Sword & Shield charge bug (Mercenary) that affected gameplay.
  • Anti-cheat and chat moderation measures ongoing per official announcements.
  • Hunt Rituals and seasonal tasks were announced as arriving the following week (Season 1 content ramp-up).

This was a server-side online update rather than a client patch, so no install was required.

FAQ

  • Where do I read official patch notes? Steam Community announcements for Mistfall Hunter (app 3282300) — the devs post full notes there.
  • How often does the game update? Frequent: launch hotfix Jul 30, balance patch Aug 6, online update Aug 7, plus weekly content drops from the roadmap.
  • Do I need to reinstall to update? No — client patches download through Steam/console store automatically.
  • Is the Battle Pass free? The Season 1 pass is free to progress; premium items are bought separately with Fate Coins.
  • Is solo mode in this patch? Not yet — it arrives mid-September with Season 1 Phase 2.
