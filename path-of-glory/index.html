Mistfall Hunter Path of Glory — How It Works & How to Enable

The Path of Glory is Mistfall Hunter's seasonal progression track. It tracks your in-game accomplishments and rewards you with cosmetics, consumables, and currency as you climb through tiers. Here is what it is, how to enable it, what rewards are available, and what to do when it's not working.

What is the Path of Glory?

The Path of Glory is a seasonal progression track — think of it like a battle pass, but focused on in-game accomplishments rather than just playtime. It is tied to the Echo of Fate season system introduced with the August 12 Soul Harvest update.

As you complete tasks, extract with loot, kill enemies, and achieve milestones during a season, you earn progress along the Path of Glory. Each tier you unlock grants a reward — cosmetics, consumables, or currency.

Key facts:

  • Seasonal reset: Path of Glory progress resets at the end of each season (see season reset guide). Unlocked cosmetics are permanently kept.
  • Free track: the Path of Glory is free — there is no paid premium track (unlike many battle passes). Echo of Fate Season Store items may cost Fate Coins, but the Path of Glory itself does not require payment.
  • Related to Echo of Fate season system: the Path of Glory and Echo of Fate Season Store are part of the same seasonal reward structure (introduced with Soul Harvest in August 2026).

How to enable the Path of Glory

The Path of Glory should be enabled by default for all players once they reach the main camp. If you don't see it:

  1. Complete the tutorial: the Path of Glory unlocks after the introductory sequence and first extraction from Hallowgrove.
  2. Check the Fate Chart: the Path of Glory is accessed through the Fate Chart in your camp. Open the Fate Chart (usually a keybind or UI button in the camp hub) and look for the "Path of Glory" tab.
  3. Update your game: the Path of Glory was added in the August 12 update. If you're playing an earlier version, you won't have it. Update through your app store (Steam/Xbox).

Google search data shows players asking "what does enabling path of glory do" — this suggests there may be an explicit "Enable" option in-game that some players miss, or that the feature doesn't auto-activate for everyone. If you find a toggle or button to enable it, toggling it on starts tracking your seasonal progress.

How to progress through tiers

Path of Glory progress is earned through regular gameplay — there is no separate grind:

  • Extracting successfully from maps
  • Completing Echo of Fate season tasks (152 tasks per season: 50 core challenges + 93 cosmetic tasks)
  • Killing enemies, bosses, and rival players
  • Looting high-value items and Gyldenblod
  • Participating in seasonal events like Soul Harvest

The more you play and extract successfully, the faster you climb the Path of Glory. Higher-difficulty maps (Cataclysm and Chaos) likely grant more progress per run than Normal difficulty.

Rewards you can earn

The Path of Glory rewards include:

  • Cosmetics: exclusive seasonal skins, outfits, weapon appearances, avatar frames, and emotes. These are usually the headline rewards at milestone tiers.
  • Consumables: Moonlight Nectar, Soul Coins, Combat Bags, and other in-game resources.
  • Currency: Fate Coins (used in the Echo of Fate Season Store), Gyldenblod.
  • Materials: upgrade materials and crafting components for camp progression.

The Echo of Fate Season Store (separate from the Path of Glory) also offers Soulbound Serpent Crystals, Holy material chests, Moonlight Nectar, and Soul Coins — these can be purchased with Fate Coins earned through the Path of Glory. See Soul Harvest guide for store details.

⚠️ Disclaimer: Exact reward tiers and quantities are not documented in public patch notes. The list above is based on the Echo of Fate season structure described in the August 12 and August 15 updates. Specific reward values may differ from what's described here.

Path of Glory not working? Common fixes

Google searches for "path of glory not working" and "mistfall hunter enable path of glory" suggest players encounter issues. Here are the most likely causes and fixes:

  • You haven't completed the tutorial: the Path of Glory only becomes available after you extract from your first Hallowgrove run and reach the main camp.
  • Season hasn't started yet: if you're playing between seasons, the Path of Glory may be inactive. Check the Fate Chart for the current season status.
  • You need to manually enable it: look for an "Enable" button or checkbox in the Fate Chart's Path of Glory tab. Some players report needing to turn it on explicitly.
  • Server-side issue: the August 12-13 server instability (cloud CPU hyperthreading issue) may have caused progression tracking to fail temporarily. If your progress didn't register, it may have been a one-time server problem — try extracting again.
  • Restart the game: sometimes a client restart fixes progression tracking bugs.

FAQ

  • What is the Path of Glory in Mistfall Hunter? It's the seasonal progression track that rewards you for playing and completing season tasks. Think of it as a free battle pass.
  • How do I enable the Path of Glory? It should auto-unlock after your first extraction. If not, check the Fate Chart in camp for an "Enable" option.
  • Does Path of Glory reset each season? Yes — progress resets, but earned cosmetics are permanent. See season reset.
  • Is the Path of Glory free? Yes. There is no paid track — all Path of Glory rewards are earnable through gameplay.
  • How is Path of Glory different from the Echo of Fate Season Store? The Path of Glory is a progression track (earn rewards by playing). The Season Store is a shop (spend Fate Coins earned from the Path of Glory and Soul Harvest events).
  • What season tasks count toward Path of Glory? The Echo of Fate season has 152 tasks per season: 50 core challenges and 93 cosmetic challenges. Completing these directly contributes to Path of Glory progress.
