Seer Build — Revenant Support & Blasphemer

The Seer is Mistfall Hunter's only true support class — healing, shields and runes. The Revenant build below is the standard trio build: a ranged support that cleanses debuffs and wins long fights. If you prefer the Mace over the Catalyst, the aggressive Blasphemer branch trades healing for damage.

Why Revenant?

The Revenant Seer is a ranged support that is still strong in 1v1 — guides call it "a Swiss Army knife." Its real value shows in trios: healing wins long fights, and Healing Art removes debuffs including anti-heal, Barbed/Bloodfly/Paralysis arrows and Sorcerer burns — a direct counter to the current ranged meta. Mobalytics ranks the Seer in A tier for trios. The trade-off is honest: it is the worst solo pick (GamesRadar ranks it C tier) because healing is wasted when you're alone, unless you play the Blasphemer branch.

Talents

The Revenant build focuses on Healing Art upgrades (cleanse and healing amount), Opportunist Strike for punish windows, and the Wind Walker support talent in trios. Exact picks depend on whether you're building for pure support or a hybrid damage-support split — see the community wiki's Seer page for the current talent trees.

Affix priority

Affixes come from gems socketed into gear. The community guides recommend this order for the Revenant:

  1. Valor 5 — reliable damage contribution while supporting.
  2. Eloquence 5 — smoother, faster skill casts (more heals and runes).
  3. Stoic 5 — survivability for a class that enemies love to dive.
  4. Elusive — as high as possible; movement keeps you alive.
  5. Optional: Ethereal / Creation / Seeker for utility-focused setups.

Blasphemer (damage) alternative

The Blasphemer branch uses the Mace for an aggressive, melee-ish support: less healing, more damage and pressure, while keeping some utility. It is the pick for players who want the Seer's toolkit without feeling like a pure healer, and it's the branch to choose if you insist on playing the Seer in solo. Both branches are viable; the community guides currently favor Revenant for coordinated trios.

Solo & trio tips

  • Healing wins long fights. In trios, out-sustain the enemy team — force extended trades where your heals matter.
  • Cleanse at the right moment. Healing Art removes anti-heal and arrow DoTs — saving a teammate from a Blackarrow stack can swing a fight.
  • Don't play solo as a beginner. Healing is wasted alone and your damage is low; learn the class in trios first (see the beginner guide).
  • Stay behind your frontline. You are a priority target for divers — keep Wind Surge ready as your escape.
  • In duos/trios, coordinate runes. Punishment + Intimidation runes on the same target create no-escape kill windows for your melee.
