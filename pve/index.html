Mistfall Hunter PVE — Is There a PVE Mode?

Mistfall Hunter is a PvPvE extraction game: you fight monsters and other players on the same map. There is no fully PvP-free mode yet — the official Solo Mode arrives mid-September 2026. Until then, PvE-focused players have Einherjar mode, Hallowgrove and lower-risk routes.

Is Mistfall Hunter PvP or PvE?

Mistfall Hunter is PvPvE. Every extraction run drops you into a map where corrupted monsters and NPC threats exist alongside rival Gyldhunters (other players). You can be ambushed and killed by a player at any moment, and when you die you lose everything you were carrying — that risk is the core of the genre.

A large share of the "Mistfall Hunter PvE" searches comes from players who want the monster-fighting, loot-grinding side without being backstabbed by other players. That is exactly what the official Solo Mode is for, and it is not here yet.

Is there a pure PvE mode right now?

No — not fully. As of Aug 11, 2026 every map still contains other players. The closest PvE-friendly options available today:

  • Einherjar mode — the boss-focused mode, where most of the danger is the encounter itself.
  • Hallowgrove — the lower-difficulty map with the weakest player competition.
  • Off-peak queueing — fewer rivals on Brandrgarde means a de-facto solo experience more often, but it's not guaranteed.

Until solo mode ships, everything you bring into a run can still be taken by other players. If you want zero-PvP extraction, wait for the official mode in September or stick to Hallowgrove.

Einherjar Mode — the boss-focused mode

Einherjar is Mistfall Hunter's boss mode: you fight powerful encounters rather than competing with other hunters over loot spawns. It is significantly more solo-viable than Brandrgarde extraction, and it is the source of the new Dried Flower Knots item (dropped by Mist Lords after the Aug 6 patch). The August 6 update also opened up the Mist Lord challenges alongside it. See our Dried Flower Knots guide for the farming details.

Aug 19 live update: entering a Mist Lord's room now grants a brief period of invulnerability, which makes the opening of boss fights safer. See patch notes.

Hallowgrove — the low-risk map

Hallowgrove is the lower-difficulty extraction map: weaker PvE enemies, looser gear requirements and the weakest player competition in the game. It's the standard recommendation for grinding gear and camp materials with minimal PvP risk — the classic farm route hits Supreme Hall, Einherjar Ship Grave and the Frostwind Path / Pioneer Gates area. See our Hallowgrove map guide and drops & farming guide.

Solo Mode (mid-September)

On August 5, 2026, Bellring Games confirmed that Solo Mode for Brandrgarde (Normal and Cataclysm) will arrive in mid-September 2026 as part of Season 1 Phase 2. In solo mode, no rival Gyldhunters appear in your queue — the map's monsters and NPC threats remain, but the PvP gamble is gone. It was the single most-requested community feature since launch. Full details in our Solo Mode guide.

Cataclysm difficulty

Cataclysm is the highest difficulty tier on Brandrgarde: higher gear requirements, tougher enemies and stricter extract rules — but higher-quality drops. When solo mode ships in September it covers both Normal and Cataclysm, so PvE-only players will eventually be able to chase top-tier loot without player competition. For now, the difficulty is still shared with rival hunters.

Soul Harvest event

Soul Harvest is the Season 1 hunt-ritual activity: Soul Cocoons, Fate Altar rituals and seasonal rewards. It's the most PvE-flavored seasonal content so far and went live with the August 12 update — it triggers inside Hallowgrove and Brandrgarde matches, with a higher chance at higher Gyldening Intensity. See our full Soul Harvest guide.

Aug 19 live update: the Scavenger Squad got a refresh mechanic — after completing a task and claiming its rewards, the required materials across all modes will refresh. See Combat Bags for how the Squad works.

FAQ

  • Can I play Mistfall Hunter without PvP? Not yet — solo mode arrives mid-September 2026. Until then every map has other players.
  • Is Einherjar mode PvE? It's boss-focused, so most of the danger is the encounter — far less player competition than Brandrgarde.
  • Is Solo Mode PvE? Yes — no rival players in your queue, but monsters and NPC threats remain.
  • When does Solo Mode come out? Mid-September 2026 with Season 1 Phase 2, for Brandrgarde Normal and Cataclysm.
  • Does solo mode cover Hallowgrove? Not announced — the confirmation covers Brandrgarde only.
