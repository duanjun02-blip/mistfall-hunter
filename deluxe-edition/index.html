Mistfall Hunter Deluxe Edition — Price, Rewards & Is It Worth It?

The Deluxe Edition costs $39.98 and includes the base game plus the Digital Deluxe Upgrade. You get 6 exclusive cosmetics (Forsaken Skeleton outfit, Shipwreck Coast background, Web of Fate earrings, Justice standing pose, Pip's Daily Life emotes, Dew avatar + Light Feather frame) and 2,500 Fate Coins. Here is the full breakdown — and whether the upgrade is worth your money.

Editions & pricing

Mistfall Hunter has two purchase options on Steam as of August 2026:

EditionPrice (USD)Includes
Standard Edition$24.99Base game only
Deluxe Edition$39.98Base game + Digital Deluxe Upgrade (6 cosmetics + 2,500 Fate Coins)

The Deluxe Edition is actually the Standard Edition + Upgrade to Deluxe Edition bundle (2 items). If you already own the base game, you can purchase the Digital Deluxe Edition upgrade separately — this appears as a DLC on the Steam store page. The standalone upgrade price is the difference: roughly $15.

Is the Deluxe Edition worth it?

The value depends on what you care about:

  • If you care about cosmetics: the Forsaken Skeleton outfit is a full-body skin visible to all players. The Dew avatar and justice pose are distinctive. For $15 extra, getting 6 exclusive cosmetics + 2,500 Fate Coins is a reasonable value compared to buying Fate Coins separately. Most extraction games charge $10–20 for a single skin.
  • If you only care about gameplay: save $15 and buy the Standard Edition for $24.99. The Deluxe Edition gives you zero gameplay advantage — no early class unlocks, no bonus stats, no exclusive weapons. You can earn Fate Coins through the Path of Glory for free anyway.
  • If you want to support the developers: Bellring Games is a relatively small studio. The Deluxe Edition is a way to show support without gaining an unfair advantage — and you get some exclusive cosmetics in return.

Verdict: worth it if you like the cosmetics and don't mind spending $15 extra. Skip if you're purely here for the extraction gameplay — the Standard Edition is the same game.

Deluxe Edition not working? Fixes

Players report issues with Deluxe Edition items not appearing:

  • Steam DLC not downloaded: in your Steam Library, right-click Mistfall Hunter → Properties → DLC → make sure "Upgrade to Deluxe Edition" is checked and installed.
  • Items appear after tutorial: Deluxe Edition cosmetics may not appear until after you complete the introductory sequence and reach the main camp for the first time.
  • Check your in-game mail: some rewards (especially Fate Coins) may be delivered via the in-game mail system rather than appearing in your inventory immediately.
  • Fate Coins balance: check your Fate Coin balance in the Echo of Fate Season Store or the in-game shop. The 2,500 coins should be credited after the tutorial.
  • PS5 / Xbox version: if you bought the Deluxe Edition on console, the DLC may need to be downloaded separately from the platform store (PlayStation Store / Microsoft Store).
  • Restart the game: if the issue persists, fully close and restart Mistfall Hunter. Some DLC items require a fresh login to sync.

FAQ

  • How much is the Mistfall Hunter Deluxe Edition? $39.98 on Steam (includes base game + upgrade). The standalone upgrade from Standard is roughly $15.
  • What is in the Deluxe Edition? 6 exclusive cosmetics (Forsaken Skeleton outfit, Shipwreck Coast background, Web of Fate earrings, Justice pose, Pip's Daily Life emotes, Dew avatar + Light Feather frame) + 2,500 Fate Coins.
  • Is the Deluxe Edition worth it? Yes if you like cosmetics — no if you only care about gameplay (it's 100% cosmetic). There is zero pay-to-win.
  • Can I upgrade to Deluxe later? Yes — the Digital Deluxe Edition upgrade is available as a separate DLC purchase on Steam.
  • What can I buy with Fate Coins? Cosmetics in the Echo of Fate Season Store and the in-game shop. See Soul Harvest guide for the Season Store inventory.
  • Does the Deluxe Edition include early access or exclusivity? No — the Standard and Deluxe editions are the exact same game with the same content. The difference is cosmetics only.
