Wave 3 — 1 million players celebration

On August 6, 2026, Bellring Games announced the game passed 1,000,000 players and released a celebration bundle. Log in before August 12, 2026, 8:00 PM (Beijing time) to receive, via in-game mail:

  • 1 × Standing Pose: Spellcasting (站姿-法术)
  • 5 × Moonlight Nectar
  • 1 × Pro Combat Bag
  • 1 × Advanced Combat Bag

Confirmed on the Steam store announcement feed and covered by Insider Gaming. Earlier waves (launch rewards) remain claimable through the September 1 login window above.

Free Season 1 Battle Pass

The Season 1 Battle Pass (Slumbering Contract) unlocks for free after 7 cumulative login days — no purchase required. If you already bought it before the free unlock, the official announcement says you will receive Battle Pass currency (Fate Coins) as compensation, which can be used toward future passes. Note the difference: Soul Coins buy cosmetics and shop items, while Fate Coins buy Battle Passes. Most players should wait for the free unlock rather than paying.

How rewards are delivered

  1. Log in to the game with the account you want to claim on.
  2. Open the main menu → In-game Mailbox.
  3. Claim the reward before it expires.

Rewards are time-limited and account-bound. If a reward doesn't appear, restart the game, confirm you logged in before the deadline, and wait a few minutes during peak server load before contacting support.

Avoiding fake codes

Because "Mistfall Hunter codes" is a hot search, scam sites publish fake redeem codes. Rules to stay safe:

  • Only trust rewards announced on the official website, Steam announcements, the official Discord and official X account.
  • Never complete surveys or download unrelated software to "unlock" a code.
  • If an offer looks too good to be true, it's a scam — there is no unlimited-codes generator.
