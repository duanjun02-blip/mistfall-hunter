Is Mistfall Hunter on PS5?

Yes — Mistfall Hunter launched on PS5 on July 30, 2026, same window as PC and Xbox. It costs $22.49 on the PlayStation Store (launch discount from $24.99), supports crossplay with PC and Xbox, and requires PS Plus for online play.

Release status & price

Mistfall Hunter is out on PS5 right now. The PlayStation Store lists it at $22.49 (regular $24.99, −10% launch discount) with the discount ending Aug 13, 2026 (01:00 GMT+8). PS Plus members may stack additional savings at checkout.

→ Mistfall Hunter on the PlayStation Store

The PS5 version is the same game as PC/Xbox: third-person extraction ARPG, three-player squads, and access to the live Season 1 (Slumbering Contract). A cross-gen question many players ask — "is this PS5 only?" — no, the same store page serves the PS5 release; there's no separate PS4 version listed.

Crossplay & progression

  • Crossplay: yes. PS5, PC (Steam) and Xbox players share matchmaking. Add friends by Player ID/nickname via the in-game friends menu (see our crossplay guide for the unlock steps).
  • Cross-progression: not officially promised. Players on Steam asked about PS5↔PC progress sync and there's no official guarantee — treat your account progress as per-platform for now, and follow official channels for changes.
  • Cross-region: region-locking details aren't officially documented; expect region matching to follow store region as usual.

PS Plus & requirements

  • PS Plus required for online co-op/PvP — the standard console requirement (Xbox similarly requires Game Pass Core/Ultimate).
  • Steam Deck is NOT supported — if you planned to play the PC version handheld, it's officially unsupported; the PS5 version obviously runs on console only.
  • Crossplay parties need the friend system unlocked — complete the tutorial, one Hallowgrove extraction, then the hub quest that opens the friends menu.

FAQ

  • When does Mistfall Hunter come out on PS5? It's already out — released July 30, 2026.
  • How much is Mistfall Hunter on PS5? $22.49 with the launch discount (regular $24.99), until Aug 13, 2026.
  • Can PS5 players play with PC and Xbox? Yes — crossplay is supported across all three platforms.
  • Do I need PS Plus? Yes, for online multiplayer, per platform convention.
  • Will my PC progress carry to PS5? Not officially confirmed — follow official announcements before buying cross-platform expecting shared progress.
