Mistfall Hunter Player Count (Live)

Mistfall Hunter hit 1 million players in its first week (confirmed Aug 6, 2026) and passed 1.5 million players by Aug 20, 2026 (official DevNote #8). On Steam alone it has been pulling 20,000+ concurrent players at peak hours (all-time peak 44,472); the morning of Aug 21 dipped to ~10.7k — an overnight low, with a 24-hour peak of 19,584. Numbers below are a snapshot from Aug 21, 2026 and change by the hour.

What the numbers mean

The launch has been one of the biggest extraction-game debuts on Steam. The 44,472 peak is notable because it "just falls short" of the all-time Steam peak for Escape from Tarkov, per Insider Gaming — for a genre that was niche on PC for years, that's a strong signal. The developers responded to the launch wave with hotfixes, a Fair Play (anti-cheat) announcement and a roadmap (Aug 5, 2026), with single-player for Brandrgarde’s Normal and Cataclysm difficulties arriving mid-September, at the midpoint of Season 1, alongside new seasonal tasks and a Hunt Ritual system.

Comparison & estimates

Third-party ownership estimates vary because they use different methodologies — treat them as rough ranges, not exact numbers:

  • VG Insights ≈ 305.8k owners
  • PlayTracker ≈ 358.5k owners
  • Gamalytic ≈ 498.3k owners

Why the gap? The game is also sold on PS5 and Xbox (crossplay is supported — see our crossplay guide), and each tracker estimates Steam-only ownership differently from publicly visible data.

FAQ

  • Is Mistfall Hunter dead? Far from it — 30k+ concurrent on Steam alone in week one, with an active Season 1 and a roadmap that includes Season 2.
  • How many people are playing right now? Check the live chart at SteamDB; our snapshot shows 10,680.
  • Do these numbers include PS5/Xbox? No. SteamDB only tracks Steam. The official 1M milestone covers all platforms.
  • Has the player count grown since launch? The all-time peak (44,472) was Aug 2; concurrent counts fluctuate, but the official 1M-player milestone came on Aug 6 — growth is still being reported.
