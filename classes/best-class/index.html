What is the Best Class in Mistfall Hunter?

Short answer for new players: Mercenary is the best starting class — it is the most forgiving and versatile. If you prefer ranged, pick Blackarrow. For solo play with a high skill ceiling, Shadowstrix is the strongest. Here is every class ranked for beginners and solos, with pros and cons.

Best classes for beginners

GamesRadar+ and GameSpot both recommend the Mercenary as the best first class: it has a good mix of mobility, offense and defense, and its Sword & Shield branch lets you block while you learn enemy patterns. The Blackarrow is the best ranged starter — fast, safe and easy to understand.

Best classes for solo play

In solo runs you need a class that is self-sufficient in both PvP and PvE. The community consensus (Mobalytics, IGN):

  • Shadowstrix — invisibility lets you slip past enemies, grab materials and extract; the ambush factor wins duels. S-tier solo, but weak against PvE bosses.
  • Blackarrow — strong in both PvP and PvE; kites melee players to death from range.
  • Mercenary (Hammer) — IGN recommends the hammer build for solo: heavy stagger combos melt PvE elites, and you can swap to Sword & Shield for player fights.
  • Sorcerer — powerful against melee but struggles in hard PvE and is easy prey for Shadowstrix. Avoid until comfortable.
  • Seer — the worst solo pick; healing is wasted and damage is low (Blasphemer branch is closer to A-tier).

A note on the current meta

Mistfall Hunter launched on July 30, 2026, and the meta is still settling. Different outlets ranked classes differently in week one:

Mobalytics (Aug 5)

  • Trio S: Blackarrow, Sorcerer
  • Solo S: Shadowstrix, Blackarrow
  • Seer A in trios, B solo

GamesRadar+ (Aug 5)

  • S: Withered Knight
  • A: Blackarrow, Sorcerer, Mercenary
  • C: Seer

GameSpot (Jun 17)

  • S: Sorcerer
  • A: Shadowstrix
  • B: Withered Knight, Mercenary, Blackarrow

Take "best class" rankings with a grain of salt: balance patches are already landing (an Aug 7 hotfix adjusted the Focus affix). The strongest advice stays the same — pick the class you enjoy; every class is viable when played well. For daily strength rankings, check our tier list page.

Quick FAQ

  • What is the best class in Mistfall Hunter? For beginners, Mercenary. For overall meta at launch, Blackarrow and Sorcerer top most trio tier lists.
  • Can I change classes later? Yes — talk to Dew after the tutorial's follow-up quest. It costs Gyldenblod and has a cooldown.
  • Is there a Rogue class? No class is named Rogue; the rogue/assassin role belongs to Shadowstrix.
  • Best class for solo? Shadowstrix (stealth) or Blackarrow (range); Mercenary hammer for a melee option.
