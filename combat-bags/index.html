How to Get Combat Bags in Mistfall Hunter

Combat Bags are the game's free preset loadouts — a full set of gear you can bring into a run without risking your own hand-built kit. There are five types (Novice, Advanced, Pro, Elite and Limited Custom), and you can get more of them from three main sources: Scavenger Squad expeditions with Twig, the Echo of Fate Season Store, and the Path of Glory progression track.

What are Combat Bags?

After you finish the tutorial, the game introduces Combat Bags — ready-made loadouts that equip a full set of gear in one action, so you can drop into a map without risking the equipment you've built up. If you die before extracting, your own kit stays safe; only the bag is consumed.

  • Consumed on map entry: a bag is only spent when you actually enter a map. Equipping or swapping bags at camp costs nothing.
  • Not editable: you can't change what's inside a bag — higher tiers simply come with better weapons, armor and healing items.
  • Per-tier storage caps: each tier has a max amount you can hold (Novice caps at 10); anything collected beyond the cap is wasted.
  • Best early-game tool: using a bag keeps your valuable gear out of harm's way while you learn each map. See our beginner guide and walkthrough for the full camp unlock order (Twig's bag production unlocks early).

All Combat Bag tiers

Open your Warehouse and switch to the Combat Bag tab to see all of them. Four sit under the Normal tab (Novice, Advanced, Pro, Elite) and the fifth, the Limited Custom bag, sits under Limited — it holds a personal loadout you build yourself and is the hardest to keep stocked.

Method 1: Scavenger Squad expeditions (Twig)

The Scavenger Squad is the steadiest supply of bags and the method guides consistently rank first. Here's how it works:

  1. Go to camp and speak with Twig (he stands next to Pip).
  2. Send the squad on an expedition to a location you've already unlocked through camp upgrades.
  3. The expedition takes a fixed amount of time and completes on its own — you can send one out roughly every hour.
  4. Return to Twig to collect the rewards, which can include Combat Bags.

Combat Bags aren't guaranteed on every trip, but the expected-reward preview shown before you send the squad tells you what may come back. Upgrading the Scavenger Squad unlocks better expeditions, which significantly raises the odds of higher-tier bags like Pro or above coming home.

Aug 19 live update: after completing a task and claiming its rewards, the Scavenger Squad's required materials across all modes now refresh. This should make the squad easier to keep cycling. See patch notes.

Twig also unlocks Combat Bag production early in the camp upgrade chain — see the beginner guide and walkthrough for where he fits in the unlock order.

Method 2: Echo of Fate & the Season Store

The Season tab offers a direct-purchase route. Once the Fate Chart is available, you can complete repeatable quests that reward Echo of Fate — then spend that currency in the Season Store to buy Pro Combat Bags and Advanced Combat Bags directly.

  • Weekly cap: you can earn a maximum of 3,000 Echo of Fate per week from event objectives.
  • Budget check: 3,000 per week is enough to pick up the Pro and Advanced bags that are on offer.
  • Time-limited: this is a seasonal event, so the exact store stock rotates when a new season starts.
  • This is the best way to target specific higher-quality tiers instead of relying on random drops.

Method 3: Path of Glory

The second Season-tab source is the Path of Glory progression track. Enable it before a run, earn progression points based on how you perform, then hit the milestone tiers to claim bags for free:

TierRequirementReward
DuskbornReach the tier, then complete 3 matchesPro Combat Bag
MistwalkerReach the tier, then complete 3 more matchesElite Combat Bag

Both tiers hand out exactly one bag each per claim, so this is a reliable but one-time-per-tier source — pair it with the Scavenger Squad and Season Store to keep a steady reserve.

Tips

  • Send the Scavenger Squad every hour. Even if you're not playing a long session, taking a minute to send a squad slowly builds a reserve.
  • Spend your weekly 3,000 Echo of Fate. It's a cap, not a target — buy the Pro and Advanced bags before the seasonal stock rotates.
  • Upgrade the squad early. Better expeditions are the main way to pull Pro and Elite bags without spending currency.
  • Save high tiers for hard maps. A bag is only consumed on map entry, so burn Novice bags on routine farming runs and hold Pro/Elite bags for Chaos and Cataclysm.
  • Don't exceed the cap. Picking up Novice bags past 10 is wasted loot — spend them down if you're at the cap.
  • Check your Warehouse Combat Bag tab after any reward to confirm what arrived and what tier caps you're close to.

Combat Bags work alongside your regular loot space — for what to actually keep from runs, see our Drops & loot guide.

FAQ

  • How do I get more Combat Bags in Mistfall Hunter? Send the Scavenger Squad on expeditions through Twig (about once per hour), buy Advanced/Pro bags with Echo of Fate in the Season Store, and reach the Duskborn/Mistwalker tiers on Path of Glory for free Pro/Elite bags.
  • How many Combat Bag tiers are there? Five: Novice, Advanced, Pro, Elite, and the Limited Custom bag (a personal saved loadout).
  • Are Combat Bags one-time use? Yes — a bag is consumed when you enter a map. Equipping or swapping bags at camp costs nothing.
  • Is there a cap on Combat Bags? Each tier has its own cap; the Novice bag stops storing at 10. There's no single overall cap across all tiers.
  • Can I use any bag on any map? No. Each mode lists allowed tiers — e.g. Hallowgrove Normal only allows Novice, Advanced and Pro.
  • Can I sell or trade Combat Bags? No — bags are bound to you and can't be sold or traded.
  • Is the Echo of Fate store permanent? No, it's seasonal and time-limited; the weekly earning cap is 3,000.
