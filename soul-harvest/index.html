Mistfall Hunter Soul Harvest (Hunt Rituals) — What We Know

Soul Harvest is Season 1's headline gameplay mode: a ritual that summons a new elite — the Soulgnawer — plus Soul Cocoons to collect, a multiplayer showdown and an exchange shop. The devs confirmed it ships with a client update "next week" from Aug 7 — so it is expected August 10–16.

What is Soul Harvest?

Soul Harvest is the seasonal gameplay feature of Season 1: Soul Hunt in Mistfall Hunter. It is tied to the Fate Chart, the season's progression and story system. In the story, the Valgr have restarted their forbidden soul experiments, turning creatures into rampaging Cocoon Puppets in a bid to seize soul power and build a False God. Dew sends hunters into the mist to track down exiled souls and contain the corruption — that gameplay is Soul Harvest.

The mode rolls out after launch as part of the Season 1 phases, not on day one. It was first confirmed in the official 1-million-players roadmap announcement on August 5, 2026, and the August 7 online update announced the client patch for "next week".

Release window

  • August 7, 2026 (official): "We will roll out one more client update next week to officially launch Hunt Rituals and start seasonal tasks."
  • Expected window: August 10–16, 2026 (the week after the announcement).
  • Status as of Aug 11: no new official announcement or patch notes yet — the update is expected any day now. We will update this page the moment it ships.

Note: "Hunt Rituals" is how the official Chinese announcement refers to Soul Harvest (狩魂仪式). Media and the community also call it the "Soul Hunt" seasonal event. Same thing.

How it works

  • Ritual in matches: in matches where the ritual is active, a new elite monster called the Soulgnawer appears. She has a slender build, a serpentine lower body and human-like facial features, and she actively disrupts the ritual.
  • Kill to complete: finishing the ritual requires killing the Soulgnawer — while rival hunters can third-party you mid-fight. That PvPvE tension is the core of the mode.
  • Soul Cocoons: you gather Soul Cocoons (灵魂之茧) during the mode; these are the main collectible/currency of the event.
  • Multiplayer showdown: a new PvP showdown mode runs alongside the ritual for exclusive event rewards.

Exchange shop & rewards

The Soul Harvest Exchange Shop trades event currency for:

  • Exclusive collectibles (unique cosmetics/vanity items).
  • Soul Coins (灵魂币) — see our How to Get Soul Coins guide for the currency's other sources.
  • Moonlight Nectar (月光蜜露) — see How to Get Moonlight Nectar.

Seasonal tasks (赛季任务) unlock at the same time, giving a separate progression path with their own rewards.

FAQ

  • When does Soul Harvest come out? Expected with the client update "next week" from Aug 7 — i.e. Aug 10–16. No exact date published as of Aug 11.
  • Is Soul Harvest live now? Not yet confirmed. As of Aug 11 there is no official announcement that the update shipped; we will update this page as soon as it does.
  • Who is the Soulgnawer? A new elite boss that disrupts the ritual — a serpent-bodied, human-faced creature you must kill to complete the ritual.
  • What do I do with Soul Cocoons? Collect them during the event and spend them in the Soul Harvest Exchange Shop for collectibles, Soul Coins and Moonlight Nectar.
  • Do I need the battle pass? No — Soul Harvest and seasonal tasks are free Season 1 content.
