All Mistfall Hunter Class Abilities

Every class in Mistfall Hunter has two weapon branches, and abilities are organized per weapon: Basic Skills, Weapon Skills and utility. Below is the full community-documented skill list for all six classes, plus the combat system rules they fit into.

Combat system basics

  • No lock-on. The devs removed target lock to raise PvP depth — aim with your camera.
  • Dodge, block, perfect block. Universal defense tools; there's no sprint key, so movement is skill-based (see our movement guide).
  • Weapon switching mid-fight. Swap between your class's two weapon branches to handle different enemies.
  • Skill types. Basic Skill (free/basic attacks), Weapon Skill (the branch's active kit), plus utility and ultimate-style slots that differ per class.

Mercenary — Sword & Shield / Hammer

The defensive all-rounder. Sword & Shield branch has ~7 skills built around blocking; the Hammer branch trades defense for damage.

  • Block / Perfect Block — raise shield; perfect timing negates and counter-opens.
  • Stacked Slash — sword combo builder.
  • Hammer Dash — hammer branch gap closer.
  • Earthshaker — AoE hammer slam.
  • Plus Shield Dash and counterattacks used in the best Mercenary build.

Sorcerer — Elemental / Stardust

The glass-cannon mage. Elemental applies fire/lightning pressure; Stardust bursts.

  • Fire Bolt — basic projectile.
  • Thunder Pierce — lightning line damage.
  • Forbidden Gate — utility/zone skill.
  • Mistwalk — mobility/evasion.
  • Meteor Charm, Stardust Tempest, Rock Blast — the Stardust core (see the Sorcerer build guide).

Blackarrow — Bows

The ranged specialist: control arrows, DoT arrows and traps.

  • Barbed Arrow — DoT bleed arrow.
  • Bloodfly Arrow — life-steal/utility arrow.
  • Paralysis Arrow — CC arrow.
  • Shadow Step — reposition/escape.
  • Scattershot — close-range spread.
  • Rapid Arrows — sustained DPS volley.
  • Spore Arrow — zone/area control.

Shadowstrix — Daggers / Dual Blades

The Rogue/assassin: stealth, shadow clones and burst. Each branch carries ~7 skills.

  • Shadow Wheel Slash — 3-hit combo; branch returns energy.
  • Flash Stride — fastest dash in the game (shorter cooldown in trio role mode).
  • Smoke Bomb — obscures enemy vision.
  • Shadow Strike — leave a shadow clone, dash in, stun.
  • Flurry Strike — rapid multi-hit.
  • Bloody Blade Dance — burst finisher.
  • Fang Rush — chase/execute.
  • Pursuit Strike — gap closing.

Full build, rotation and counters: → Shadowstrix (Rogue) build guide.

Seer — Revenant / Blasphemer

The support/control caster with two rune branches.

  • Punishment Rune — damage/control rune.
  • Binding Rune — root/CC rune.
  • Healing Art — sustain/support.
  • Wind Surge — mobility/push.

Withered Knight — Greatsword / Polearm+Shield

The tanky bruiser. Greatsword focuses on wither/burst combos; the polearm+shield branch is currently considered weak and officially slated for buffs.

  • Breakthrough Charge — shield charge gap closer.
  • Radiant Retribution — counter/punish.
  • Intervene — ally protection.
  • Sacred Bulwark — defensive stance.
  • Parry — perfect-timing counter.

Sources: community wiki class pages (Mercenary / Sorcerer / Seer / Shadowstrix / Blackarrow / Withered_Knight), Mobalytics Shadowstrix guide, Mobalytics Sorcerer guide, and official Steam marketing. Skills change with patches — verify in-game after updates.
