Mistfall Hunter Drops — Loot Guide & Farm Routes

Drops are everything in Mistfall Hunter: you scavenge gear, materials and currencies, then extract or lose them. This guide covers how drops work, what to keep vs sell, the best farm routes on Hallowgrove and Brandrgarde, higher-difficulty drops, and where specific key items come from.

How drops & extraction work

  • Sources: monsters, chests, elite enemies, bosses, and rival players you defeat — everything they carried becomes a drop.
  • Extract to keep: find and kill a rare Returner Woodling for a Soul of Return, then leave the map before the timer ends. If you die, you drop everything — and other players can loot your body.
  • Rarity: items and materials come in rarity tiers, from grey common junk up to Epic and Legendary quality. Golden Ciphers, for example, drop as Legendary-quality scrolls during the Gylden Rush event.
  • Bag space: your Combat Bag limits how much you can carry out — more bag slots mean more profit per run.

What to keep vs sell

The short version from IGN's loot guide: always grab the highest-tier materials and gear you can, while collecting as much Gyldenblod as you can hold. In practice:

  • Keep: gear that upgrades your class, gems, Soul Catch Nets, ingots, camp-upgrade materials, potions, and key common buff materials. High-tier materials are worth more later than their sell price now — selling them early is usually a mistake.
  • Sell: low-tier junk and duplicates. When your bag is full, drop the grey items first.
  • Unidentified items: pick these up — identifying them can roll into something valuable (Golden Ciphers can come from unidentified rolls).
  • Where to sell: Cazarro's Shop for quick cash on unwanted gear and consumables; Pip's Auction House for high-rarity, player-demand items once it unlocks around level 5.

Higher difficulty = better drops

Difficulty directly affects drop quality. Cataclysm and Chaos-mode runs drop higher-quality gear and materials than Normal runs. Mobalytics' gearing guide calls Chaos Mode one of the best ways to gear up: go in, loot everything, sell what you don't need on the auction house. The trade-off is that the hardest difficulties are also the most dangerous — every death costs you the whole bag.

Best farming routes

  • Hallowgrove (low risk): the classic route balances camp materials, rare loot and elite enemies. Priority POIs: Supreme Hall (a boss and great loot), Einherjar Ship Grave (locked area with locked chests), and Frostwind Path / Pioneer Gates (good loot and close to the other two). One revive is allowed on this map, and PvE enemies are low difficulty.
  • Brandrgarde (high value, high risk): denser monster zones and better chests, but stronger competition from rival hunters. Run a chest loop and extract early — consistency beats greed.
  • Einherjar Mist Lords: the Aug 6 patch made Dried Flower Knots drop from Mist Lord encounters here — a key item for the new Chaos challenges.
  • Low-PvP "rat" route: wear cheap green/blue gear, avoid PvP hotspots, prioritize treasure containers over fights, and extract early. It's the safest way to bank Gyldenblod while learning.

Making money from drops

  • Auction house is the safest money-maker: Mobalytics recommends it as the most reliable way to turn drops into Gyldenblod — list high-rarity gear players actually want instead of vendoring it.
  • Fast cash: sell duplicates and weak pieces at Cazarro's Shop for immediate Gyldenblod.
  • Extract early: one survived extract is worth more than a full clear that ends in death. The moment you have a valuable drop, prioritize getting out.

FAQ

  • Do I lose my drops when I die? Yes — you drop everything you're carrying, and other players can loot your body.
  • What's the best map to farm? Hallowgrove for low risk, Brandrgarde for higher value — see the routes above.
  • Do higher difficulties drop better loot? Yes — Cataclysm and Chaos runs drop higher-quality gear and materials.
  • What should I keep? Highest-tier materials and gear, gems, camp-upgrade materials, key items, and as much Gyldenblod as you can hold.
  • Can I farm without PvP? Not fully until solo mode (mid-September), but Hallowgrove and Einherjar runs are far lower risk. See our PVE guide.
