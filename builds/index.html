Best Mistfall Hunter Builds for Every Class

Builds in Mistfall Hunter combine a weapon branch, active skills, talents and gear affixes. Strong builds focus on one weapon setup instead of splitting into all-rounders. Here is the best build for each class, cross-checked across guides from Skycoach, Mobalytics and the community wiki.

Build principles that apply to every class

  • Focus one weapon setup. Splitting between both branches makes a build mediocre.
  • Elusive is the best solo affix. Better dodging directly improves survivability.
  • Valor is the reliable damage affix for nearly every class.
  • Utility skills usually beat pure damage skills — they drain the opponent's resources and make them easier to kill.
  • Everything costs Gyldenblod. Farm efficiently and upgrade the Gyldenblod Pool early.

Mercenary — Sword & Shield (Gladiator)

The best Mercenary build avoids the Hammer entirely and focuses on gladiator-style fighting: blocking, perfect blocks, Shield Dash and counterattacks. It is not a button-mashing build, but it is more forgiving than most.

  • Core skills: Shield Dash (gap closer + defense), Stone Breaker Slash.
  • Playstyle: bait attacks, perfect-block, then punish; the build is strong in solo duels but less impactful in trios.

Sorcerer — Stardust (burst mage)

The Stardust Sorcerer trades slow ranged pressure for powerful burst. Meteor Charm is the main damage skill because you can chain it with instant casts; Stardust Tempest adds damage and slows; Rock Blast baits dodges.

  • Skills: Meteor Charm, Stardust Tempest, Rock Blast; defensives Phantom Step / Shadow Veil / Windcraft / Cryptic Ward.
  • Talents: No Escape, Quagmire, Storm Impact (optional Enchanted Ward, Hurricane).
  • Affixes: Eloquence 5 → Elusive → Vitality → Range → Fervor.

Full skill-by-skill breakdown, solo tips and budget variants: → Sorcerer build guide.

Blackarrow — Hunter's Arrow

The best Blackarrow build uses Hunter's Arrow: a fast, DoT-heavy playstyle built around spacing and chasing. It is almost a melee-range bow build and is seriously strong, especially for newcomers.

  • Skills: Barbed Arrow, Bloodfly Arrow, Paralysis Arrow, Shadow Step, Scattershot, Rapid Arrows (swap Paralysis → Spore Arrow vs melee classes).
  • Talents: Pursuit Mark, Mark Pursuer, Blood Infection, Powerful Scattershot, Battle Fed, Lasting Pain, Dodge Weapon Shot, Laceration.
  • Affixes: Vitality, Elusive, Valor, then Curse (strengthens Barbed/Bloodfly DoTs).
  • Counters: a good Seer or Withered Knight; keep moving and stay just out of melee reach.

Shadowstrix — Dual Blades (Pressure)

The Shadowstrix is fast and deals huge damage; several guides call the Dual Blades build one of the strongest in the game. You engage from stealth, use Crow Storm for control, and deal damage with Spinning Slash — a positioning and damage skill that synergizes with many talents.

  • Skills: Spinning Slash, Crow Storm, Strike Throw, Bloody Blade Dance, Fang Rush.
  • Talents: Wound Buildup, Ambush, Haste, Combat Euphoria, Unstoppable, Blackout, plus Crow Storm range/AoE upgrades.
  • Affixes: Elusive, Vitality, Valor, then damage or stealth-focused affixes.
  • Note for "rogue build" searchers: this IS the rogue build — Shadowstrix is Mistfall Hunter's assassin class.

    • Full skill breakdown, rotation and rogue counters: → Shadowstrix (Rogue) build guide.

Seer — Revenant (support)

The Revenant Seer is a ranged support that is still strong in 1v1 — "a Swiss Army knife". Healing Art restores health and removes debuffs (including anti-heal, Barbed/Bloodfly/Paralysis arrows and Sorcerer burns); Wind Surge is the escape and even a kill tool.

  • Skills: Wind Surge, Healing Art, Punishment Rune, Intimidation Rune, Burst Rune (swap Wind Surge → Death Ward in trios; consider Wind Walker Totem).
  • Talents: Healing Art cleanse/healing upgrades, Opportunist Strike, Wind Walker support talent for trios.
  • Affixes: Valor 5, Eloquence 5, Stoic 5, Elusive as high as possible (optional Ethereal/Creation/Seeker).

Withered Knight — Greatsword (Wither Burst)

The best Withered Knight build is the Greatsword Wither Burst build: apply Wither, extend it, and trigger execute damage through heavy attacks. (Polearm & Shield is currently weaker and expected to be buffed.)

  • Skills: Breakthrough Charge, Radiant Retribution, plus Parry (solo) or Thorn Guide (trios).
  • Talents: Greatsword Specialization, Crime, Bad Karma, Righteous Creed, Long Range, Whiplash, Dominance (Legacy optional).
  • Affixes: Burst, Valor, Tenacious, Aegis.
  • Core combo: Crime makes Greatsword executes not consume Wither; Bad Karma adds debuffs when Wither lands — lock them down and keep pushing.
