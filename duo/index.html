Mistfall Hunter Duo Mode — Is Duos Available?

Straight answer: No, Mistfall Hunter does not have a dedicated duo / duos mode. The game supports solo play and 3-player squads only — there is no 2-player queue or duo matchmaking. Here is the official word, why players want duos, and what you can do when you have only one friend online.

Official word from Steam

The Steam store page for Mistfall Hunter is clear about team sizes. The description reads:

"Stalk the mist as a lone wolf, or band together in a three-player squad."

There is no mention of a 2-player option anywhere in the game's description, system requirements, or features list. The game's multiplayer tags also confirm: Online PvP and Online Co-Op — but no duos.

Steam features list: Online PvP · Online Co-op · Steam Achievements · In-App Purchases · HDR · Family Sharing · Kernel Level Anti-Cheat.

Workarounds for 2-player teams

If you and one friend want to play together, here are your options:

  • Queue as a duo into trios (fill with a random): form a 3-player squad where the third slot is filled by matchmaking. This is the most common approach — you and your friend coordinate while the random third plays independently. Downside: you can't control who the third is, and PvP coordination with a random teammate is difficult.
  • Play solo on the same map: drop into the same map separately and try to meet up in-game. This is risky because there is no friendly-fire protection — you could accidentally kill each other — and you can't revive each other. But some players do this on Hallowgrove where PvE enemies are easier.
  • Wait for solo mode (September 2026): when the Brandrgarde solo mode launches (currently planned for mid-September), you won't need a squad at all. See our solo mode page for details.
  • Community pressure: the volume of duo-related searches suggests strong demand. If enough players request duos on Discord or the Steam Community Hub, the developers may add it in a future season. The game already has infrastructure for both solo and 3-player — adding a 2-player queue is technically feasible.

Best duo combo ideas

While there is no official duo queue, here are synergies players theorize for two-person coordination:

  • Withered Knight (Greatsword) + Blackarrow: the Knight tanks and parries up front while Blackarrow deals ranged DPS from behind — classic front/backline setup. See Blackarrow builds and Withered Knight guide.
  • Shadowstrix + Sorcerer: high mobility flanking + AoE magic burst. The Shadowstrix can scout and harass while the Sorcerer times big damage windows.
  • Mercenary (Sword & Shield) + Seer (Support): durability + healing/buffs. A Mercenary with a shield build absorbing damage while the Seer provides sustain is a nightmare to kill.

FAQ

  • Can you play Mistfall Hunter with 2 players? Not officially — the game supports solo and 3-player squads. There is no 2-player matchmaking queue.
  • Is duos coming to Mistfall Hunter? Not announced. The developers have not mentioned duos in any patch notes or DevNotes (as of August 2026). Given community demand, it's possible in a future season.
  • How do I play with one friend? Queue as a trio and fill the third slot with matchmaking. Or drop into the same map solo and try to meet up.
  • Why doesn't Mistfall Hunter have duos? Likely a design choice — the extraction loop, revive mechanics, and map size are balanced around 1 or 3 players. Adding duos would require rebalancing.
  • Can I solo in Mistfall Hunter instead? Yes — solo play is fully supported now, and dedicated solo mode arrives in September. See solo mode page.
