How to Get Soul Coins in Mistfall Hunter

Soul Coins are the premium shop currency in Mistfall Hunter — they buy cosmetics and items like Moonlight Nectar (200 Soul Coins each). There is no daily quest that showers you with them, but two big confirmed sources give you about 1,200 free Soul Coins right now.

The 1,200 free coins math

Reddit player NimblePasta calculated the Season 1 pass total: you can redeem 700 Soul Coins after completing the Season 1 Battle Pass. Add the 500 launch celebration coins mailed by the devs, and that's 1,200 Soul Coins. If Season 2's Battle Pass costs the same, you may be able to unlock it for free again with saved coins.

Important: Soul Coins are not the Battle Pass currency. Passes are bought with Fate Coins. Don't spend your Soul Coins on a pass — save them for shop items.

What to spend Soul Coins on

  • Moonlight Nectar (200 Soul Coins each) — needed to refine Star Silver for the Watchtower upgrade. Many players recommend this as the best use if you're progressing camp upgrades.
  • Cosmetics — emotes, poses, skins and avatar items from the Mall.
  • Future Battle Passes — only if the next pass accepts Soul Coins; Season 1 uses Fate Coins.

Advice from the community: Soul Coins are scarce, so avoid impulse cosmetic purchases early. A few Moonlight Nectar buys can unblock the Watchtower progression chain faster than anything else.

FAQ

  • Can I buy Soul Coins with real money? The Mall sells items for Soul Coins, and Soul Coins themselves are tied to premium/event systems — treat them as a limited resource and prioritize the free sources above.
  • Do Soul Coins carry between platforms? Rewards are tied to the game account you log in with; claim on the account you plan to keep.
  • Is there a daily quest for Soul Coins? Not confirmed as of Aug 7, 2026. The reliable routes are the login reward and the Battle Pass.
  • Where's the mailbox? Main menu → In-game Mailbox. Rewards expire, so claim them before the listed deadlines.
