How to Sprint in Mistfall Hunter (There Is No Sprint Button)

Straight answer: Mistfall Hunter does not have a sprint button. It was requested on day one, the devs said they'd "consider it seriously," and it still isn't in the game. Movement speed comes from Dodge and each class's mobility skills — here's the full list and how to chain them.

Confirmed: no sprint key

Players asked for a sprint button on launch day, and the thread was answered by other players arguing that sprint would make melee combat feel worse — nobody pointed to a keybind, because none exists. The official marketing only ever described "fluid action, no lock-on, combos and weapon switching" — never sprint. In a June 2026 feature-request thread, a developer replied "Thanks for your feedback. We shall consider it seriously" — a consideration, not a promise, and nothing has shipped since launch (July 30, 2026).

Bottom line: if you searched "how to sprint in Mistfall Hunter" because a guide or video mentioned it, you're not doing anything wrong — the feature simply isn't in the game. Movement is tied to abilities instead.

Movement tips

  • Dodge isn't just for dodging — it's your main travel boost. In open space, dodge-jump chains beat walking everywhere.
  • Don't spam dodge in combat — the animation locks you briefly and enemies will punish it. Save it for reads.
  • Use class mobility on rotation — every class has at least one movement skill; keeping it off cooldown before a fight is a positioning win.
  • Pre-position instead of sprinting — since there's no sprint, map awareness matters more: know extraction routes before you need them.

FAQ

  • Will sprint be added? The devs replied "we shall consider it seriously" to a June 2026 request, but as of Aug 7, 2026 there is no sprint and no announced patch for one.
  • Is there a horse/vehicle? No — all traversal is on foot with Dodge and skills.
  • Which class is fastest? Shadowstrix has the fastest movement kit (Flash Stride + Shadow Step).
  • Is Dodge on the same key as roll? Dodge is your universal evasive roll; there's no separate sprint key to bind.
