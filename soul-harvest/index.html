Mistfall Hunter Soul Harvest Guide: How It Works & Rewards

Soul Harvest is Season 1's headline gameplay mode — a match-made ritual where you collect Soul Cocoons, activate a Fate Altar and purify corrupted souls for rewards. It went live in the August 12 update alongside a new Fate Chart stage. Here's the full mechanic breakdown.

What is Soul Harvest?

Soul Harvest is the seasonal gameplay feature of Season 1: Soul Hunt in Mistfall Hunter, tied to the Fate Chart — the season's progression and story system. It's a special operation launched by Dew and the Gyldhunters to mend the Web of Fate: the Velgr are trying to create a False God by warping creatures with Soul Cocoons, and you have to defeat the mutated monsters and perform a purification prayer to return their disordered souls to the Web of Fate.

The mode was first confirmed in the official 1-million-players roadmap announcement (Aug 5), promised in the Aug 7 online update for "next week", and went live with the August 12 update.

Release & status

  • Live now (Aug 12, 2026): Soul Harvest shipped with the August 12 Live Update — a no-downtime update, after which you update your client via the app store. A new Fate Chart stage arrived alongside it.
  • Also in the patch: a world-chat blocklist and real-time voice chat on Xbox, plus the full class balance pass and bug-fix list on our Patch Notes page.

Note: "Hunt Rituals" is how the official announcement refers to Soul Harvest (狩魂仪式). Media and the community also call it the "Soul Hunt" seasonal event. Same thing.

How it works

Per the official August 12 announcement, here's the exact mechanic:

  • Triggers inside matches: while Soul Harvest is active, matches in Hallowgrove and Brandrgarde have a chance to trigger it. When it triggers, Soul Incubators carrying Soul Cocoons spawn randomly on the map, along with a Fate Altar for the purification prayer.
  • Higher Gyldening Intensity = higher trigger chance: push the map difficulty up if you want to see Soul Harvest more often.
  • Cocoon resonance: any hunter carrying a Soul Cocoon resonates with other cocoons — you can see every other cocoon's location on the map, but your own position is revealed to the other cocoon holders too. Risk vs reward.
  • Start the ritual: once the match has been running for a while, head to the Fate Altar and submit 2 Soul Cocoons to activate it and begin the ritual.
  • Richie's team reward: you can also give 1 Soul Cocoon to Richie, the in-match merchant, to unlock a special Soul Cocoon reward for your team.

What about the Soulgnawer? Community wiki coverage describes the ritual's elite as the Soulgnawer — a serpent-bodied, human-faced creature that disrupts the ritual; the official Aug 15 announcement confirms defeating the Soulgnawer to claim Soul Harvest rewards. The Aug 12 notes describe the threat as "mutated creatures warped by Soul Cocoons."

Rewards & exchange shop

Two ways Soul Cocoons turn into rewards:

  • Fate Altar ritual: submit 2 cocoons to start the purification — completing it pays out the seasonal rewards tied to the new Fate Chart stage.
  • Richie's special offer: hand 1 cocoon to the in-match merchant to unlock a special team reward (per the in-game tutorial guide).

Soul Harvest Exchange Shop: the seasonal shop trades event currency for exclusive collectibles, Soul Coins (灵魂币) and Moonlight Nectar (月光蜜露), per the official roadmap. See our How to Get Soul Coins and How to Get Moonlight Nectar guides for those currencies' other sources.

Mistchaser Shop: completing Soul Harvest runs earns exclusive tokens exchangeable at the Mistchaser Shop for rare rewards — including the Season-exclusive Holy-quality collectible Soulbound Serpent Crystal, a Holy Material Selection Chest, Moonlight Nectar and Soul Coins (official announcement, Aug 15, 2026).

Fate Chart Shop: now also sells Moonlight Nectar and a Legendary Material Selection Chest, with more rare rewards added as the season progresses (Aug 15, 2026).

Login reward: log in before August 22, 2026, 00:00 UTC+0 to claim Moonlight Nectar x5, Pro Combat Bag x2 and Advanced Combat Bag x2 via in-game mail (Aug 15, 2026). For every way to stock more bags, see our Combat Bags guide.

Seasonal tasks (赛季任务) run alongside Soul Harvest, giving a separate progression path with their own rewards.

FAQ

  • Is Soul Harvest out now? Yes — it went live with the August 12 update. No-downtime patch; then update your client via the app store.
  • How do I trigger Soul Harvest? It triggers randomly in Hallowgrove and Brandrgarde matches while the event is active — higher Gyldening Intensity raises the trigger chance.
  • What do I do with Soul Cocoons? Submit 2 at a Fate Altar to start the ritual, or give 1 to Richie (the in-match merchant) for a team reward.
  • Can other players see me carrying a cocoon? Yes — resonance reveals every cocoon's location (including yours) to all other cocoon holders. Watch your back.
  • Who is the Soulgnawer? The ritual's elite threat — described in the official notes as mutated creatures warped by Soul Cocoons. We'll confirm the exact in-game name soon.
  • Do I need the battle pass? No — Soul Harvest and seasonal tasks are free Season 1 content.
