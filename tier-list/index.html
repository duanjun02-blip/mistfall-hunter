Mistfall Hunter Tier List — All 6 Classes Ranked

The meta is young — the game launched July 30, 2026 and the Aug 6 patch already nerfed Blackarrow and buffed Withered Knight. This page ranks all six classes for trios and solo using the most current guides, and shows where major outlets disagree so you can make your own call.

On this page
  1. Trio tier list (Mobalytics)
  2. Solo tier list (Mobalytics)
  3. How other outlets rank them
  4. The consensus

Trio tier list

Based on Mobalytics (updated Aug 5, 2026). In trios, ranged damage dealers keep up constant pressure from the backline, which is often stronger than melee — so Blackarrow and Sorcerer top the list.

TierClassWhy
SBlackarrowThe go-to ranged DPS: kites, melts from high ground, safe from melee. Aug 6 patch removed knockback on uncharged Steel Arrow shots — slightly less control, same damage.
SSorcererPowerful but punishing if spells miss; great spell versatility in any team.
ASeerHeals teammates through long fights and hard PvE bosses; hard for beginners.
AShadowstrixExcellent vs. squishy teams; needs coordination, struggles in PvE.
BMercenaryTanky melee option; Sword & Shield counters melee teams.
BWithered KnightGood if you parry reliably; struggles against ranged classes. Aug 6 buff cut the Energy cost of Sprinting Slash and Withering Mark.

Solo tier list

Solo play rewards self-sufficient classes. Stealth and range win; healers lose value.

TierClassWhy
SShadowstrixStealth + dash to close distance or escape any fight; the ambush king.
SBlackarrowStrong in PvP and PvE; kites melee players to death. The Aug 6 Steel Arrow nerf matters less in solo duels.
AWithered KnightVersatile and great in PvE; struggles against ranged classes. Better after the Aug 6 Energy-cost buff.
AMercenaryBest melee DPS option solo; reliable in most situations.
ASorcererGood vs. melee, but must watch danger constantly; weaker in PvE.
BSeerHealing is wasted solo; Blasphemer branch is closer to A.

How other outlets rank them

Week-one rankings genuinely disagree — that's normal for a fresh meta. Here's where the major sources landed:

GameSpot (Jun 17, 2026)

  • S: Sorcerer (crowd control + burst in both PvE and PvP)
  • A: Shadowstrix
  • B: Withered Knight, Mercenary, Blackarrow

GamesRadar+ (Aug 5, 2026)

  • S: Withered Knight (damage potential)
  • A: Blackarrow, Sorcerer, Mercenary
  • B: Shadowstrix
  • C: Seer

Mobalytics (Aug 5, 2026)

  • Trio S: Blackarrow, Sorcerer
  • Solo S: Shadowstrix, Blackarrow
  • Seer: A in trios / B solo

Why the disagreement? Ranged classes are strong in coordinated play, melee classes reward player skill more, and community tier lists (r/MistfallHunter) update daily as players learn matchups. GameSpot's article also predates launch.

The consensus

  • Ranged DPS is king in week one: Blackarrow and Sorcerer appear in every S-tier.
  • Shadowstrix is the solo specialist: highest ceiling, hardest to play.
  • Mercenary is the safe pick: never top, never bottom — the reliable all-rounder.
  • Seer is the double-edged sword: best support in trios, worst solo pick.
  • Withered Knight is the sleeper: ranked S by GamesRadar, B by Mobalytics — strong if you master slow, wide attacks.

Sources: Mobalytics tier list, GameSpot, GamesRadar+. Rankings change with patches — the Aug 6 update nerfed Blackarrow and buffed Withered Knight, and an Aug 7 hotfix adjusted the Focus affix. Snapshot Aug 9, 2026.
