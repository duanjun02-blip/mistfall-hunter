How to Get Dried Flower Knots in Mistfall Hunter

Dried Flower Knots are a new key item added in the August 6, 2026 patch. They drop from Mist Lords in Einherjar mode and are used to directly activate the Mist Lord's Einherjar mode in Gyldening Intensity: Chaos. The August 6 client update specifically increased their drop rate and opened up the Mist Lord challenges.

What is a Dried Flower Knot?

Dried Flower Knots are a key-type item (item database category: Key) added on August 6, 2026. The in-game item description reads:

"Used to directly activate the Mist Lord's Einherjar mode in Gyldening Intensity: Chaos."

Their lore flavor ties into the game's spirit world: "A knot made of dried flowers through which the living pay respects to the dead, it may awaken lingering attachments deep within the memories of departed spirits."

How to get them (confirmed)

Per the official August 6 patch notes, "Dried Flower Knots will now start to drop" when you challenge Mist Lords in Einherjar mode. In practice:

  • Source: defeating Mist Lords — the toughest bosses in Einherjar mode.
  • Difficulty: the item drops on the mode's hardest encounters. The August 6 update increased the drop rate and opened up the Mist Lord challenges alongside it.
  • Requirement: you need to successfully overcome the powerful Mist Lord boss fights — these are endgame-level encounters, not early-game drops.

The August 6 patch summary from Bellring Games: "Dried Flower Knots will now start to drop. Challenge Mist Lords in Einherjar mode and prove your strength!" (IGN wiki patch notes, Aug 5-6, 2026).

What they're used for

  • Direct activation: a Dried Flower Knot lets you directly activate the Mist Lord's Einherjar mode in Gyldening Intensity: Chaos — skipping the normal unlock path for that boss encounter.
  • Endgame loop: they function like other cipher keys in Mistfall Hunter — separate items that don't go to camp NPCs for rewards, but instead unlock content (in this case, the Chaos-difficulty Mist Lord fight).

In short: if you want to farm Mist Lords at the highest intensity without grinding the standard unlock conditions each time, Dried Flower Knots are the shortcut item to stock up on.

Farming tips

  • Run Einherjar mode on the highest difficulty you can clear. Drops are tied to the Mist Lord encounter — the harder the clear, the better your odds.
  • Bring a boss-killing build. Check our builds and tier list pages for high single-target setups — Mist Lords are sustained damage checks.
  • Don't ignore the surrounding Gyldenmist modifiers. Higher Gyldening Intensity changes the fight; the Chaos activation is specifically tied to that intensity bracket.
  • Team up for consistency. Einherjar Mist Lord clears are significantly more reliable with a coordinated squad; if solo, prioritize survivability over DPS.

FAQ

  • Where do Dried Flower Knots drop? From Mist Lords in Einherjar mode, added in the August 6, 2026 patch.
  • Are they a guaranteed drop? No — they're a drop-rate item. The Aug 6 update increased the drop rate, but clears are still the gate.
  • Can I buy or craft them? No shop or crafting source has been confirmed; they're an Einherjar-mode reward only.
  • What is Gyldening Intensity: Chaos? The highest intensity bracket for Einherjar mode; a Dried Flower Knot directly activates the Mist Lord fight there.
  • Do they expire? No expiry has been announced — unlike event currency, they're a permanent key item.
