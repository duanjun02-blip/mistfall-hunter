How to Get Moonlight Nectar in Mistfall Hunter

Moonlight Nectar is a scarce camp currency in Mistfall Hunter: it refines into Star Silver, which funds the long Watchtower upgrade chain. There is no reliable farm for it — but there are four confirmed sources, including a free batch from the 1-million-player event.

All confirmed sources

SourceAmountDetails

Journey milestonesSeveralTask → Journey Overview: completing milestone objectives rewards Moonlight Nectar. Free and repeatable as you progress.

Important: there is no confirmed farming method. Players on r/MistfallHunter asked the same question after launch and the consensus is that Nectar only comes from the sources above — the Watchtower is one of the longest camp upgrades in the game, so budget Nectar toward it early.

Fastest free options right now

  1. Claim the event mail. If the 1M-player Wave 3 reward (5 Moonlight Nectar) is still active, open your in-game mailbox and claim it before the deadline.
  2. Open Task → Journey Overview and check which milestone is closest. Journey rewards are the main repeatable free source.
  3. Work the Season 1 pass. Redeem the two Nectar nodes on the free track; they don't require premium currency.

What Moonlight Nectar is for

The entire purpose of Nectar is the camp progression chain. In lore, it's "a rare nectar of the three realms that no Woodling can resist — used to trade with them for ore" (GamerGuides). In gameplay, that means:

  1. Take Nectar to the goddess statue at camp and ask Ashe to refine it into Star Silver.
  2. At the Curio Shop, spend 7,200 Star Silver on the Starsilver Spindle.
  3. Use the Spindle to upgrade the Watchtower landmark (near Cazarro's shop) — each tier unlocks the next Watchtower level plus legendary emote rewards.

Related: short on Soul Coins? See how to get Soul Coins — about 1,200 are free right now from login rewards and the Season 1 pass.

FAQ

  • Can I farm Moonlight Nectar from mobs? Not confirmed as of Aug 7, 2026. Community consensus is that Nectar is event/quest/pass gated, not a drop.
  • Should I buy it for 200 Soul Coins? Only if you're specifically grinding the Watchtower chain. Otherwise save Soul Coins — they're the premium shop currency.
  • How many do I need? Enough to hit the 7,200 Star Silver required for the Starsilver Spindle, plus whatever the next Watchtower tier asks.
  • Is the event Nectar still claimable? Event waves have deadlines — check the official Steam announcements and claim mail before it expires.
